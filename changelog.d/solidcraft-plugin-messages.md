### Formats

- When the SolidCraft plugin can't convert a file, its message says what to do first, states the
  1 GiB input limit, and names only the formats the plugin converts. FreeCAD files are left to
  SolidCraft's own reader, which keeps more of their history.
- Unresolved appearances stay visible in the plugin's loss report. Size-limit advice now
  distinguishes the shared per-file cap from the plugin's combined assembly limit.
